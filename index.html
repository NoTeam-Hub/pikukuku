Our Story

Meet Piku & Kuku

Piku and Kuku aren't chefs — they're two baby boys, the sons of the woman whose kitchen this all started in. Long before either of them could hold a spoon, their mother was already up early most mornings, sun-drying chillies and mixing spice the way her own mother had taught her.

When it came time to name the jars she'd started sharing with neighbours — and then selling online — there was only one choice: the pet names she calls her boys at home. Every jar that leaves this kitchen still carries that same care, tasted twice and sealed only when it's good enough for her own family's table.
